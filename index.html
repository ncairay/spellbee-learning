The Case of the Jade Dragon

A shop that robbed itself. A camera that saw everything and knew nothing. A detective who has been bored for a hundred and thirty-one years.
Sam Wong-Watson is eleven, lives above her family's noodle stall in Sai Ying Pun, and writes down things that don't add up. On the night of the typhoon she finds a silver pocket watch in an old trunk and steps out of her storeroom into London, 1895. A Hong Kong mystery for readers 9–12.

一間自己打劫自己嘅舖頭。四部乜都影到、乜都唔知嘅閉路電視。一個悶咗一百三十一年嘅偵探。
阿 Sam 十一歲，住喺西營盤家人嘅大牌檔樓上，有本簿專記「唔合理嘅事」。打風嗰晚，佢喺舊皮篋搵到一隻銀懷錶，一踏出雜物房，已經係 1895 年嘅倫敦。英文原創，特別為香港 9–12 歲小朋友寫得清清楚楚。
